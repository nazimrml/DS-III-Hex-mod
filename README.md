## Features

- **True Dark Souls II Stat Requirements**: Spells demand dual Intelligence and Faith investments identical to Dark Souls II (e.g. Climax requires 30 INT / 47 FTH, Affinity requires 40 INT / 24 FTH).
- **Authentic DS2 Damage Scaling**: No weak tickle damage. High-tier hexes feature true spell buff motion values:
  - **Dark Orb**: Deals 700–780+ damage at endgame levels.
  - **Great Resonant Soul**: Deals 1,000+ damage.
  - **Climax**: The ultimate hex nuke dealing 1,500+ damage.
- **Sunset Staff Added**: Features S-rank scaling in both Intelligence and Faith, native staff casting animations, and high dark spell buff.
- **Natural In-Game Progression**: Spells, catalysts, and attire unlock organically through the Shrine Handmaid as you discover and turn in Umbral Ashes.
- **1 Attunement Slot for All Hexes**: Streamlined attunement slot requirements for build flexibility.
- **Lore-Tailored Boss Resistances**: 25 bosses custom-balanced according to their elemental origins and lore (from -20% for Lightning/Frost deities to 85% for Midir). Regular enemies maintain a clean 0% neutral baseline.
- **No External Launchers Needed**: Works directly by replacing `Data0.bdt`. Run the game normally via Steam or `DarkSoulsIII.exe`!
