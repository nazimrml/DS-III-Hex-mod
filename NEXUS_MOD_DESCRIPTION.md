# Dark Souls II Hexes in Dark Souls III

A complete, lore-grounded overhaul that brings the authentic Dark Souls II Hex magic system to Dark Souls III. Experience true Dark sorceries and chimes with original DS2 INT/FTH requirements, high motion-value damage scaling, natural ash-based shop progression, and lore-tailored boss resistances.

---

## Highlights

- **Original DS2 Stat Requirements**: Every single hex retains its exact Dark Souls II Intelligence and Faith requirements.
- **Authentic DS2 Damage Scaling**: No more tickle damage. Dark Orb hits for 700+ at endgame with high-tier dark catalysts, Great Resonant Soul delivers 1,000+, and Climax acts as the ultimate nuke (1,500+ damage).
- **Sunset Staff Included**: Features S-rank scaling in both Intelligence and Faith, with native staff casting animations and optimized dark spell buff.
- **Natural Progression via Umbral Ashes**: Hexes, catalysts, and attire are cleanly unlocked at the Shrine Handmaid as you advance through the game and turn in Umbral Ashes.
- **Lore-Accurate Boss Resistances**: Bosses have custom hex resistances tailored to their lore and design (from -20% for Lightning/Frost gods up to 85% for Midir). Regular enemies remain at clean 0% neutral resistance.
- **Zero External Launchers Required**: Launches natively with standard `DarkSoulsIII.exe` or Steam just by dropping in `Data0.bdt`. Fully compatible with ModEngine 2 as well!

## Installation

### Method 1: Direct Drop-in (Recommended - No Mod Engine Needed)

1. Navigate to your Dark Souls III **Game** directory:
   - Steam default path: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\DARK SOULS III\Game\`
   - (Or right-click Dark Souls III in your Steam library &rarr; **Manage** &rarr; **Browse local files** &rarr; open the **Game** folder).
2. *(Optional)* Create a backup copy of your original `Data0.bdt`.
3. Copy the `Data0.bdt` file from this download and paste it into your `Game` folder, overwriting the original.
4. Launch the game normally via Steam or `DarkSoulsIII.exe`!

### Method 2: ModEngine 2

1. If you use ModEngine 2, copy the included `mod` folder directly into your ModEngine 2 folder.
2. Launch using `launchmod_darksouls3.bat`.

---

## Uninstallation

- **Method 1**: Restore your backup `Data0.bdt` or right-click Dark Souls III in Steam &rarr; Properties &rarr; Installed Files &rarr; **Verify integrity of game files**.
- **Method 2**: Delete `Data0.bdt` from your ModEngine 2 `mod` directory.
